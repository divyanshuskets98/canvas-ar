Generative AI video (optional, free)

      
Free, runs on this PC (needs an NVIDIA GPU). 1) Install ComfyUI Desktop. 2) In ComfyUI open Workflow → Browse Templates → Video, pick an image-to-video template (e.g. LTX-Video) and let it download the models. 3) Run it once to check it works. 4) Workflow → Export (API) and paste the file's contents below.
