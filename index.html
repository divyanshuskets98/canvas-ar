How it works

  1. Learn – the canvas image is turned into a tracking target in your browser (no upload).
  2. Understand – on-device vision finds sky, water, foliage, snow and light sources (flames, bulbs, windows, sun), and a small neural net names objects.
  3. Compose – every simulation scores itself against what was found; the best fits switch on and are placed exactly on the detected regions.
  4. Anchor – image tracking locks the simulation to the physical canvas, so it keeps position, scale and perspective as you move.
